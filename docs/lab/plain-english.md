# Player text

Write full grammatical sentences for instructions, state, history, dialogue and errors. Short control labels may name an action or object. Say literally what happens. Use one name for each thing. Explain a game term on first use. Introduce one new idea at a time. Use familiar words and short sentences. Aim for grade 7 instructions; dialogue may be richer but must remain grammatical. State uncertainty without guessing motives. Cut unnecessary words.

Keep the Turkish setting and person/place names. Render common Turkish nouns in plain English. At most two flavour words may remain, each explained within its first sentence. Keep faith a quiet background detail, using HF research wording. Preserve culture, rules and difficulty. Write game prose at its source. Keep canonical admission labels and HF-generated narration unchanged. Use display glosses for HF text and historical saves. Current-version exports must replay; older exports may be refused with a clear instruction to start a new game. Keep diagnostics behind exact `?lab=1`. An ordinary error must name the failed action and preserve existing saves and receipts. It may point to retry or Feedback without promising recovery.

The frozen shared reading checker flags source candidates, including internal and historical strings. Its counts are heuristics, not reading grades or counts of rendered sentences.
